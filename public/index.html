      About this forecast

      For the first one to three hours, the rain already on radar is moved along its measured path, with a range of possible paths to show the uncertainty. Weather models take over gradually as that projection loses accuracy. How fast that happens depends on how quickly the rain near you is changing right now, and models that matched the radar over the past hours count for more.

      Radar: RainViewer (national weather services). Weather models: Open-Meteo (ECMWF, DWD, NOAA, UK Met Office, Météo-France, ECCC, JMA, CMA and others). Map © OpenStreetMap contributors.
