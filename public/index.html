      Sources & method

      For the first one to three hours, the rain already on radar is moved along its measured path, with a range of possible paths to show the uncertainty. An AI model trained on Indian radar adds its own prediction once it has proven itself. Weather models take over gradually as the radar projection loses accuracy; models that matched the radar recently count for more. A forecast gives probabilities, not certainties: for warnings, follow the India Meteorological Department.
